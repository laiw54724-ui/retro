# Community code of conduct

Retro welcomes contributors and users regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability. Be constructive, specific, and considerate. Critique work without attacking people; respect privacy, consent, creative ownership, names, and pronouns.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, stalking, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private content or credentials, and repeated disruption after a maintainer request.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the account contact listed on the repository profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical; retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project spaces, and public events where someone represents Retro.
